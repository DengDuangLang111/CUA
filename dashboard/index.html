Findings

What the experiments established

findingevidence
Length predicts failurePass falls monotonically with instruction length on both corpora (official 58%→16%, v8 33%→11%); at matched lengths they pass equally — the v8–official gap was length mix, not grading.
Official pre-opens the workspace85% of OSWorld-Verified, 82% of OSWorld-V2, 47/47 impress tasks launch or open the app in config. Cold starts test a first mile official never tests.
Thinking was silently offThe agent flag was a DashScope-only no-op and vLLM 0.25 renamed the response field; every run before 2026-08-09 ran thinking-blind. Fixed — 99.7% of calls now carry captured reasoning.
Repair beats rejectOne ~200-token instruction rewrite per fixable gate failure: 7.6 vs 1.9 keeps/batch (4×) at equal gates, zero semantic drift in graded fields.
Decks cannot be built in-VMtxt→odp conversion exists on no machine (Writer has no presentation export). Presentation fixtures now ship as prebuilt real binaries, base64-embedded.
Loop-lock is not (mainly) preservePreliminary: 6/13 v11 failures loop-lock under no-preserve, ≈ v8's share under preserve. Cold-start / model-inherent explanation gains.
Opus 5 writes tighter graders~1.5× fewer coverage gaps per task than Opus 4.6 under a fixed third-party judge (sonnet-4.6).
